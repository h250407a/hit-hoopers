Whether you want to represent HIT in inter-university varsity leagues or just stay fit and make friends on the court, HIT Hoopers welcomes you.

How Registration Works

  1. Complete the online form
    Provide your basic student details, playing background, and preferred role.
  2. Attend orientation & open run
    Join us on Tuesday or Thursday at 5:00 PM at the Main Sports Hall for a light shootaround.
  3. Collect your official club jersey
    Get added to our WhatsApp team group, squad roster, and match fixtures.
