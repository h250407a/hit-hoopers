About HIT Hoopers

A student community built around basketball, belonging, and ambition at Harare Institute of Technology.

OUR STORY

Everyone gets a chance to play

HIT Hoopers was established to provide an active, welcoming space for students across all faculties to train, compete, and forge lifelong friendships through basketball. From open court shootarounds to inter-varsity championships, our team represents the true spirit of HIT athleticism.

Join Our Community

Our Mission

To create an inclusive basketball community that develops skills, confidence, teamwork and healthy physical habits for all HIT students.

Our Vision

To be the premier collegiate basketball programme in Zimbabwe, combining academic excellence with championship-calibre sporting passion.

Our Objectives

  • Conduct bi-weekly structured skill development workouts.
  • Compete in ZUSA and tertiary basketball tournaments.
  • Promote health, wellness, and mental fitness on campus.
  • Foster inclusive student leadership and camaraderie.
